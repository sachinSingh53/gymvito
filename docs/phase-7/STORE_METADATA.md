# GymVito 1.0.0 Store Metadata Draft

## Listing

- App name: GymVito
- Short description: Local-first gym members, memberships, dues, receipts, reports, and encrypted backup.
- Category: Business
- Languages: English, Hindi
- Support scope: Android phones and tablets; device-local single-gym operation

## Full description

GymVito helps a gym owner manage plans, member profiles, memberships, invoices, recorded payments, dues, receipts, reports, CSV exports, and encrypted backups on one Android device. Core work remains available without a GymVito account, cloud database, or synchronization service. The app uses the device time zone and supports English and Hindi.

Payment entries record what the owner says was received; GymVito does not process online payments or confirm bank settlement. Owners control exports and backups through Android system destinations and are responsible for securing the device, exported files, and backup passphrases.

## Data-safety draft

- No advertising SDK, tracking SDK, remote analytics, or automatic operational-data upload is included.
- Gym, member, membership, invoice, payment, and settings data are stored locally.
- Exports, receipts, and backups leave the app only after an owner-initiated system share/document action.
- Production database and backup encryption require native-build verification before listing submission.
- The final Play data-safety form must be checked against the generated signed artifact and every configured third-party destination.
